An r that grows a leaf

The symbol is the first letter of the name, so the logo and the word are one thing.

The stem is the exact stem of Bricolage Grotesque, our headline typeface, so the mark and the letters share one weight. Its left edge keeps rising and sweeps into a leaf whose tip clears the tall b and l. That gives the word three high points and a rhythm you can recognise from across a room.

A crescent vein splits the leaf. On dark grounds its lower half turns Lime, the colour Rebloom keeps for recoveries.
